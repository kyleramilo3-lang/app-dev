# My Favorite Series: Dolce Amore

## Overview
**Dolce Amore** (English: *Sweet Love*) is a 2016 Philippine romantic drama television series starring Liza Soberano and Enrique Gil. The story revolves around Serena, a young Filipina adopted by a wealthy Italian couple, and Tenten, a hardworking and poor adopted boy from Tondo, Manila, who cross paths and find love despite their different worlds.

---

### Key Information
* **Genre:** Romance, Comedy, Drama
* **Country of Origin:** Philippines / Italy
* **Lead Stars:** Liza Soberano (*Serena Marchesa*) & Enrique Gil (*Simon "Tenten" Ibarra*)
